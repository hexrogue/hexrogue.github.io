I am a fullstack developer based in Malaysia. I started in 2020 with no laptop, just an Android phone, and learned by building. I build software that replaces manual work with automated systems. My focus is on internal tools, CRM platforms, document automation, and APIs that integrate into existing workflows.

I am also studying, and finished last semester with a 3.8 CGPA.

## What I Do

I take business problems and build working software. That usually means:

- **Automation**: Replacing repetitive manual tasks with scripts, scheduled jobs, and integrated systems
- **Internal Tools**: Dashboards, CRMs, and admin panels that let teams move faster without leaving their workflow
- **Document Automation**: PDF generation, form wizards, and template systems that eliminate copy-paste work
- **API Integration**: Connecting WhatsApp, email, databases, and third-party services into cohesive systems

## Recent Work

At **SKC Education Global**, I built a full-stack admissions CRM that handles lead management, study plan generation, WhatsApp automation, and APEL.A portfolios. The platform replaced a scattered workflow of spreadsheets, email, and paper forms with a single system that multiple advisors use daily.

I also built **WhatsAPI**, a Go-based WhatsApp gateway running in Docker, which gives the CRM full messaging and call capabilities without relying on third-party SaaS.

On the side, I build tools like a stock analysis dashboard with a custom weighted scoring engine, a deterministic career gap analyzer, and a terminal-based prayer time calculator that implements spherical trigonometry from research papers.

## How I Work

- **Stack**: Go for backend tools and APIs, Python for scripting and data work, TypeScript/Next.js for web apps, PostgreSQL for data, Cloudflare Workers for edge logic
- **Tools**: Neovim, Tmux, Git, Linux (Arch on btrfs with zstd is my daily driver, borg handles backups, aria2c downloads, rsync moves things around)
- **Approach**: Write clean, explicit code. Understand the whole stack. Ship working software.

## Security

I take security seriously. Inputs are validated, dependencies are kept minimal, and credentials are never hardcoded. I prefer fewer lines of code with clear behavior over complex abstractions.
