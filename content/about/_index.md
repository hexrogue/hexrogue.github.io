I am Muhammad Nuh, a fullstack developer based in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia. I started in 2020 with no laptop, just an Android phone, and learned by building. Six years later, I still learn the same way: pick a problem, go down the stack, and ship something that works.

I build software that replaces manual work. That usually means internal tools, CRM platforms, document automation, APIs, and the glue that connects systems nobody designed to talk to each other. I also spend a lot of time below the app layer: Linux, networking, cloud infrastructure, and embedded devices.

I am not interested in being the person who knows one framework. I am interested in being the person who can find the problem, learn the missing layer, and leave behind something the next person can operate.

## What I Do

I take business problems and turn them into working software. The common shapes:

- **Automation**: scripts, scheduled jobs, integrations, and workflows that remove repetitive work.
- **Internal tools**: CRMs, dashboards, admin panels, and systems built for the team that actually uses them.
- **Admissions and assessment systems**: candidate portals, portfolio builders, document review, scheduling, payments, and the status trail around them.
- **Document automation**: PDF generation, form wizards, templates, and the unglamorous plumbing that makes them reliable.
- **API integration**: WhatsApp, email, databases, and third-party services connected into one coherent system.
- **Platform work**: Docker, Linux, cloud services, networks, and the deployment details that decide whether a project survives contact with reality.
- **Embedded and network projects**: ESP32, wifi, BLE, sub-GHz radios, and the occasional driver that refuses to cooperate.

## How I Work

- **Go** for backend tools, APIs, and anything that should be a small binary.
- **TypeScript and Next.js** for web apps that need to feel fast and stay maintainable.
- **Python and Bash** for scripts, data work, and the glue around the edges.
- **PostgreSQL and SQLite** for data, chosen by what the problem actually needs.
- **Linux, Docker, and cloud** for deployment, with an eye on cost and operational overhead.
- **Neovim, Tmux, Git, and a terminal** because they are fast, predictable, and mine.

My approach is simple: understand the whole path, write explicit code, and ship something that works. I prefer fewer moving parts over clever abstractions. I care about security, performance, and the kind of simplicity that survives a bad Tuesday.

## The Path Here

- **2020**: started coding on an Android phone with no laptop, using Termux and a lot of stubbornness.
- **2023**: moved to Arch Linux as a daily driver and learned to fix my own bootloader before breakfast.
- **2025**: joined SKC Education Global as a fullstack developer and started building the admissions CRM and WhatsApp gateway.
- **2026**: built APEL.A, the first APEL.A admissions system in Malaysia, and SKC Central, the group's internal operations platform. Went deeper into wifi, BLE, sub-GHz, ESP32, and the layers below the browser.
- **Now**: studying software engineering, building internal tools, going deeper into Kubernetes and cloud infrastructure, and keeping a public [research notebook](/research/) on edge computing.

## Beyond Web Dev

I run a homelab: OpenWrt routers, segmented networks, WireGuard tunnels, Proxmox, and self-hosted services behind reverse proxies. Wireshark is usually open somewhere. I learn by breaking things in a lab and then writing down what broke.

I also do security work for fun: web pentesting with Burp Suite, binary exploitation, reverse engineering with Ghidra and radare2, dynamic analysis with GDB, network mapping with Nmap, and Metasploit for lab exploitation. On the device side, I root Android phones and unlock bootloaders. Breaking things is how I learned to build things that hold up outside the lab.
